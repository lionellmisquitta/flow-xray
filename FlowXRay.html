The readout
drag any box to arrange the layout — arrows follow, exports too
Reading the numbers · 89 in = how many times tickets entered that status (a ticket that loops back counts again). 15h typ = the median stay — half the tickets moved on faster, half slower. It's the median, not the average, so one ticket stuck for a month can't distort the picture. Arrow colour grades every hop against your own process, not an outside benchmark: green is your faster half, amber your slower half, red your worst 20%. An arrow's colour belongs to the status the ticket was sitting in — the wait happened before the next team ever saw it. Completed hops only tell half the story, so the still in flight panel below counts the tickets parked in each status right now (measured against the newest timestamp in your file) — that's where today's backlog actually lives.
